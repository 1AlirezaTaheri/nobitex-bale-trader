### Actual access ledger and limits

Read this ledger before the bibliographic summaries below. An A grade describes the cited research type, **not successful retrieval of every paper here**. Public search integration returned HTTP 402. Many publisher/NBER/SEC pages returned 403; Crossref returned 429 for part of the bounded metadata batch (no retry storm). Standard PDF libraries were absent and no library was installed. The read tool could not render PDFs for this model. A local stdlib literal-text extractor recovered partial prose from R1/R3/R4/R6; it does not recover every font, formula, table, layout or chart. The full literature-review goal is therefore **PARTIALLY VERIFIED**, with explicit gaps, not a claim that all cited full texts were read.
